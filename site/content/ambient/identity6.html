lamplight, alive

no border and no flat fill anymore. bise is a light in the mist: the glass has no edge, it fades into your desktop; the light comes from inside it, warm in the middle where you read, and goes out toward the edges. around it, three depths of mist and dots of light: far ones, seen through the blur; near ones, floating in front, softer and larger. move your mouse: they slide at their own depth. and it all moves, slowly, and each mood moves its own way. watch it live first, then every feature.

your note: “on voit encore un poil trop les borders et le background, ça fait un peu aplat. ce serait aussi intéressant de l'avoir en mouvement, un vrai motion design. … la sensation de volume aussi, de profondeur.”

no edgethe glass fades out over its last 26 px: no line, no corner, no box. what you see is light, not a panel.
volumelight from inside (brighter in the middle, warmer at the top), a soft dusk under it instead of a shadow, and mist on three planes that the glass blurs or doesn't.
readablethe text sits where the inner light is strongest: ink on light at 7:1 or more over the darkest painting. the motion never passes under a word.

who bise is

who bise is: the lamp left on

bise is the lamp someone leaves on in the next room. you don't look at it, but you know someone's home, and you can call out to it. it's a presence, not a performer: it keeps watch while agents work, leans in when you talk, taps your shoulder once when it needs you, and goes back to its corner. the mist is its body: how its dots of light breathe, warm and settle is how bise feels, so you read its mood from the corner of your eye, before any word.

calmit never hurries you. the light moves slowly, the words are few. nothing blinks, nothing bounces.
attentivewhen you talk, it turns toward you: the light opens and brightens. it says back what it heard before it acts.
discreetit speaks when something needs you, and once. news is said in one line, then it fades back to the status.
warm, drya little humor when things go well, never on a failure. it says "i", calls you "you". the agents are its hands: it names them, but it's always bise who talks to you.
how bise talkslowercase, short, the result first. perf is done: signup 4.1 s → 0.9 s. got it. dark mode first, then the banner. cookies asks: keep the banner on mobile?
how it never talksno exclamation marks, no "great question", no "i'd be happy to", no emoji, no fake feelings. never oops! something went wrong 😅, but release failed: npm said 403. i'm on it.
a light touch, when things go well3 done, 0 waiting. the house is quiet. back already? nothing needs you. at most one per day's worth of news, so it stays a smile, not a tic.
never a ghostit doesn't appear from nowhere, flicker in the dark, or whisper. it fades in where you left it, always the same place, the same warm color. at night it lowers to an ember, it never goes cold.
