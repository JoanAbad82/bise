the glass, the pearl

back to the first glass interface (direction A: dark smoked glass, white text, bise pink), with everything we learned since: nothing on screen at rest, a menu-bar sign that says if work is running and how much waits for you, the capsule only when there's something to see, and bise's moods. bise's body is now the mist pearl, in pink: a ball of pink mist with no rim, its edge breaking into dots of light. it lives in the menu bar, and it's what you see first when the glass opens.

bise, the pearl

mood

pink mist with a cream light inside, lilac in its shadows (the brand's pink and cream, the lilac of the first glass orbs). no rim: it's a presence, not an object. at 18 px it still reads as a soft pink light, and it moves only when something is going on.

at reststill, a little dim. it's there; it asks for nothing.
leans inyou hold fn: it brightens, swells a little, its mist speeds up with your voice.
answersit glows while bise speaks, in time with the words.
keeps busyagents work: its mist turns slowly, a whirlpool. the only sign of work.
taps your shoulderneeds you: hotter pink, one slow pulse, then it holds.
a small noddone, or a page ready: a warm cream glint, a few seconds.
says it straightfailed: the mist stops and dims. no drama.
keeps watcha meeting, a call, you're away: it lowers to a lilac dusk. never cold.

the menu bar: its states

at a glance: is anything running (the mist moves, or it's still), how many things wait for you (a pink count), and news for a moment. nothing blinks.

a 46 s film at real size. the pearl in the menu bar is the only thing that stays; the glass comes out of it only when there's something to see. play/pause, click the timeline, ← →.

when the glass shows, and when it goes

at restnothing on screen. the pearl in the menu bar, still.
you hold fn (or fn space)the glass comes out of the pearl while you talk, stays while bise answers, goes back in 4 s after. your pointer on it keeps it open.
something needs youonce, for 8 s, under the pearl; then it goes back in and leaves the count. answer any time: fn 1 / fn 2, a click on the pearl, or hold fn and say it.
while agents worknothing on screen. the pearl's mist turns. a click on it (or fn 0) shows what's going on.
done, failed, a page readynever the glass. a glint or a ▲ on the pearl for 8 s; pages wait in the pearl's menu under “for you”.
hide it, any timeesc, a click elsewhere, or fn . sends it back at once. hidden stays hidden until you call it or the next question comes.
meetings, calls, full screenthe glass never comes out by itself and bise doesn't speak; the pearl dims to lilac and the count waits.
the menua click on the pearl: the glass with the agents and what waits. a right click: needs you, for you, type to main, hide bise, quit.
