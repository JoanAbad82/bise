the keys, fn first

voice comes first, but your hands are on the keyboard. one idea holds it together: fn is bise's key. hold it alone to talk; press it with another key to do something from anywhere, without opening a window. once the panel is open, plain keys work, like in the terminal. try them: space stands for fn on this page (a browser can't see fn).

open points

  • macOS already uses some 🌐 / fn combos (🌐E emoji, 🌐F full screen, 🌐A the Dock, 🌐N notifications, 🌐C control center, 🌐Q quick note, fn+arrows page up/down). the picks here avoid those; the ones marked to verify I haven't checked on a Mac yet.
  • fn's own setting: “press 🌐 to: do nothing” in System Settings › Keyboard, or the first press opens emoji or dictation. bise would check it at first launch and show you the switch.
  • keyboards without fn (most non-Apple ones): a second key to hold, right ⌥ or caps lock?
  • answering from the notification: fn + 1 / 2 needs bise's own notification (the glass one), not macOS's: a macOS notification can't take a digit. bise's would appear in the same place, top right.
  • talking to an agent directly (fn + ⌥ + digit) breaks “always main”. it's there because the terminal has it (⌥1-9); say if you'd rather keep voice for main only.
