the agent that lets itself be forgotten

you asked for an identity that's more bise's own: always there, gone until you need it, almost your OS, the thing you use your computer through. five directions. each one answers the same question first: at rest, can you even see it? then it shows what happens when you hold fn, main answers, something needs you, an agent is done. same words everywhere, so you compare looks, not copy.

each film loops every 17 s: rest · hold fn · main thinks · main speaks · needs you · done. the side says what it is, its mark, colors, type, how it moves, and what its pages (what agents make) look like.

  • what stays in all five: the :* (in the menu bar, and the kiss at the end of a turn), the words (you, main, agents, the inbox), pink as the only color that means “for you”, fn to talk.
  • what changes: where bise lives on the screen, and how much of a “window” it is. glass (round 2) is one box top right; these go further toward no box at all.
  • they mix: e.g. the constellation's stars at rest + subtitles to speak; or the cursor at rest + the breath when you talk. tell me what you like in each.
