bise, painted

round 2 changes bise's own brand, not only the app's. bise isn't a terminal anymore, so the ASCII, the pixels and the sideways face give way to paint: brush, light, mist, stars. it's still the same bise: a kiss and a north wind, lowercase, warm, a little cheeky. it shows up the way light comes into a room: a presence that reassures, never a ghost. four directions, every one painted stroke by stroke in your browser, so you watch it appear and leave.

your brief: “le côté stellaire, le côté ambiant, le côté brumeux, le côté apparition, mais comme une présence rassurante, pas comme un fantôme. … le côté peint, brush … une idée à choper en mode artsy et pas trop tech.” the paintings from ~/lab/paint-canvas set the bar: oil on a toned ground, visible strokes, warm light.

the markthe sideways pixel face, ASCII → the :* painted: two dabs and a star of three strokes. it is still typed as :* in text.
the lookterminal brut, paper + pencil → paint on a toned canvas: brushed light, visible strokes, mist, a few stars. the terminal keeps its own look.
the colorone pink accent on gray → a palette of pigments. rose madder stays the one color that means “for you”; the rest are earths, dusk and light.
the typeNewsreader + JetBrains Mono + Caveat → Instrument Serif to speak (large, calm), Newsreader to read, Inter Tight for small labels. mono only for code.
the motionblink, gust, pop → things are painted in and fade like light: strokes lay down, glazes deepen, the light leaves. nothing bounces.
what staysthe name and its story, lowercase, the voice (“i”, short, warm), the four words (you, main, agents, the inbox), fn to talk, the kiss at the end of a turn.
  • how they relate: they share one world (an evening, warm light, a north wind). lamplight is the most reassuring and the easiest to live with all day; nocturne keeps the stars you liked in the constellation; the mist is the most “apparition”; the stroke is the most graphic, and the best logo.
  • they mix well: the stroke as the mark and the logo, nocturne or lamplight as the presence on the desktop, the mist as the transition when bise arrives and leaves.
  • next, once you pick: the mark finalized (and painted for real with paint-canvas, if you want the oil look in the app icon), the brand book rewritten (site, README, app, the kit's two surfaces), then the app and pages restyled. the code doesn't change shape: the kit's tokens and the orb are swapped.
