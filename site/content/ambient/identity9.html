bise, like Spotlight

your dogfood notes, as a design. one bar in the middle of your screen, like Spotlight: you talk or type in it, and everything bise has for you is a list of rows under it, each with its key shown. the pearl floats on its own to the left of the bar, with nothing behind it. round 8's glass, pink and moods stay; the layout, the type and the motion are new, aimed at Siri-level polish.

your notes: “il faut toujours que l'espèce d'orbe soit affiché sans background à côté de toute interface … on devrait partir sur quelque chose spotlight like avec plein de raccourcis clavier … une UI aussi polished que ce que peut faire apple avec siri.”

what changes, from your notes

the pearl floatsno square, no background: it sits outside the glass, to its left, and grows a little while you talk. same in the menu bar.
a question reads like a questionits text without the options repeated, at most 4 lines (⌘ ⏎ for the rest). the choices are one-line rows with their key, never tall boxes.
everything by keyboardevery row shows its key. digits pick, ↑↓ move, ⏎ opens, tab starts an agent, ⌘⏎ asks main, esc closes. the mouse works too (it was a bug that it didn't).
fn from anywherehold fn with nothing open: the bar and the pearl come up, listening. (a bug in the dogfood build.)
one place, the middletalking, answers, questions, the inbox and the agents all live in the same bar, at the same place, like Spotlight. the menu bar's pearl stays the always-on sign.
Siri-level polishSF at 23 / 16 / 15 / 12, 44 px rows, 26 px radius; the bar appears in a quick fade with a 4 px move down (0.16–0.2 s), never a grow, words land with a short blur; nothing jumps.

the keys, frozen for the build

every key here is shown on the UI where it works. fn + key works from any app (bise's event tap eats it, so fn 1 never types a 1). plain keys work only when the bar has the keys: when you opened it (fn space, fn 0, fn i, fn p, fn ,, a click on the menu-bar pearl), after you talked, or after you clicked a pushed card. what bise pushes on its own (a question, drafts, the hello) never takes the keys.
