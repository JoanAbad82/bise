the marble, out of the way

two of your notes, one answer. bise is a marble: a small floating ball of warm painted smoke, lit from inside. it's bise's body everywhere: in the menu bar, at 18 px, it's all you see when nothing needs you. when bise has something to say, or you talk to it, the light comes out of the marble and opens under it (round 6's surface), then goes back in. at rest, nothing on your screen.

your notes: “at rest, la ui devrait carrément disparaître … une menubar icon qui nous indique s'il y a du travail en background ou pas, le nombre d'inbox messages … même quand ça travaille, il faudrait qu'on puisse cacher la UI” and “the marble … pourrait être assez compatible avec une entité de peinture un peu flottante.”

the entity: five marbles

all five are the same character: lamplight smoke that moves with bise's mood (slow at rest, faster with your voice, a whirlpool while it works, rose when it needs you, olive when something's done, an ember when you're away). they differ in how painted, misty or pointillist they are. pick one (a click): the film and the menu-bar icons below use it. each one is shown big, then at 64 and 32 px, and at 18 px in a dark and a light menu bar.

when the light comes out, and when it goes back

at restnothing on screen. the marble in the menu bar, still.
you hold fn (or fn space)the light comes out of the marble and opens under it while you talk. it stays while bise answers, then goes back in 4 s after the answer ends. your pointer on it keeps it open.
something needs youit taps your shoulder once: the question opens under the marble for 8 s, then goes back in and leaves the count. answer any time: fn 1 / fn 2, a click on the marble, or hold fn and say it.
while agents worknothing on screen. the smoke in the marble turns slowly: that's the sign. a click on it (or fn 0) opens what's going on and what waits for you.
done, failed, a page readynever the capsule. an olive glint (done, ready) or a small ▲ (failed) on the marble for 8 s. a page is also in the marble's menu, under “for you”.
hide it, any timeesc, a click anywhere else, or fn . : it goes back in at once. bise finishes saying the answer unless you hold fn again. hidden means hidden: it comes out again only when you call it, or for the next question.
meetings, calls, full screenthe light never comes out by itself, and bise doesn't speak. the marble dims to an ember; questions wait in the count.
the menu (a click on the marble)the capsule opens with the agents and what waits for you. a right click: type to main fn space, hide bise fn ., quit bise ambient, plus “needs you” and “for you”.
