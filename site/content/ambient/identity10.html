your agents, from the bar

round 9's bar, now with your agents in it. type to find one, or fn a for all of them. arrow onto one and its preview shows on the right: what it does now, its last actions, what waits on you, its last report. ⏎ or a click opens its side panel on the right edge of the screen: its whole history, live, where you can answer it, write to it, follow it or open it in the terminal. everything you see reacts to a click, and the usual Mac keys work (⌘Q, ⌘W, ⌘F, ⌘C, ⌘,).

your words: “dans le menu Spotlight, je puisse sélectionner un agent et quand je descends dessus, je vois sur la droite … ses dernières actions, un petit résumé de ce qu'il est en train de faire … et quand je clique, ça m'ouvre un side panel sur la droite qui soit interactif avec l'historique … une vraie UI. il manque beaucoup de click events … il n'y a pas de commande Q pour quitter.”

the choices, and my pick

A · preview inside the bar (my pick)the bar widens to 860 for lists (it opens at that width, it never grows while shown): the list on the left, 360; the preview on the right, 500. one glass, one place, like Raycast. ⏎ then opens the side panel and the bar goes.
B · the side panel peeksthe bar stays 700; the selected agent's side panel shows read-only on the right edge as you arrow, and ⏎ makes it live. one place for an agent's details, but two glasses on screen at once, and the panel flickers as you arrow fast.
why Ayou look at one spot while you browse; nothing moves at the edge of your eye; the panel only comes when you ask for it. B is the fallback if 860 feels too big on a 13" screen.
the side paneldocked to the right edge, full height under the menu bar, 440 wide, the same glass; the pearl floats to its left. it stays until esc / ⌘W, and you can keep working in your apps meanwhile: it takes the keys only when you click in it or open it.
clicks everywhereevery row, chip, keycap hint, tool row, page, card option and button reacts to the pointer (hover tint, a tooltip with its key) and to a click (= its key). the audit is below.
⌘Q and the Mac keys⌘Q quits bise ambient only when bise has the keys (the bar or the panel is in front), never your front app. ⌘W closes the panel, ⌘F finds in it, ⌘C copies, ⌘, settings.

the data, for amb-core

what each view needs, by field. names are suggestions; the shapes are what the UI draws. everything live comes as hub events on the capsule's stream (no polling).

viewfieldsnotes
agent row (fn a, search)name · status (working · idle · waiting · blocked · done · failed) · title (the TUI title line: what it does now) · purpose (one line, for search) · since (status changed at) · waits (count of his cards on it) · followed · order hintsorted: waits > 0 first, then working by since, then idle, done, failed. search matches name, title, purpose; returns match ranges for <mark>.
previewrow + now (title, as a sentence, ≤ 140 chars) · actions[3..5] {at, text one line, kind: tool · message · report · page · land} · waiting[] {card_id, question} · last_report {kind: progress · done · failed · blocked, text first sentence, at} · pages[] {id, title, v}one request per selection, debounced 80 ms; cached; refreshed by its events while shown. action texts are summaries (“ran the e2e tests · 3 failed”), never raw tool output.
