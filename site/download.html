install

  1. 1open bise-mac-arm64.dmg from your Downloads.
  2. 2drag bise to Applications.
  3. 3open bise from Applications.macOS asks once if you want to open an app from the internet: Open. after that, bise updates itself when you quit it or while you're away, and your agents keep working through it.
