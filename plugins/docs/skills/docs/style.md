# How a document should read

A document is read top to bottom by someone busy. Say the point first,
then what backs it, in plain words.

## Structure

- Write the headings first, as a list. Read only them: they should give
  the outline of the argument. Then fill each section.
- The title says the point (`Q3: revenue up 4%, churn is the risk`), not
  the topic (`Q3 Report`).
- The first paragraph is the summary: what happened, what it means, what
  you ask for. Someone who stops there knows enough.
- Headings in order: Heading1 for sections, Heading2 inside them; never
  skip a level. No heading for a section of one short paragraph.
- Short paragraphs: one idea, three to five sentences.
- Lists for things that are parallel (steps, options, items). Numbered
  when order matters. Not for an argument: that is prose.
- A table when the reader compares numbers across rows; units in the
  header row; at most 5 or 6 columns.

## Words

- Plain words, short sentences, active voice: `We missed the target by
  3%`, not `The target was not fully achieved`.
- Real numbers with their unit and their source, not "significant growth".
- No filler: no "In today's fast-paced world", "It is worth noting that",
  "delve", "leverage", "robust", "seamless", no closing paragraph that
  repeats the summary. No emoji.
- No placeholders left in: `TODO`, `TBD`, `[insert …]`, lorem ipsum.
  `check()` flags them; a gap you cannot fill is a question in your reply.
- His document: keep his voice and his terms. Change what was asked,
  track it, and leave the rest alone.

## Format

- Use the document's styles (Title, Heading1-2, ListParagraph, Quote),
  never bold text dressed as a heading: styles make the navigation pane,
  the table of contents and the viewer's outline work.
- Bold for a few words that must stand out, not whole sentences. Italic
  for a title of a work or a term on first use. No underline except
  links. No colored text.
- The default font and size of the template or of his document.
- Images: `width` up to the text width (about 450 pt on A4), an `alt`
  that says what it shows.

## Before you say done

- `check()` is clean, or each warning is named in your reply
  (`open_changes` on his document is expected: he reviews them).
- Read the title, the headings and the first paragraph in order: they
  stand on their own.
- Tell the user in a line or two what the document says, or what
  changed and where (`section 2: notice period 30 → 45 days, tracked`).
