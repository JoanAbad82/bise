# Landing in this repo

Land first, end-to-end after, on main only. Before `sb land`: the fast checks only, `tests/gate.sh quick` GREEN on your sha (clippy, your crates' tests, PROOF, typecheck, lint, vitest); `sb land` runs no check of its own (no `[land] check` in bise.toml), so your quick gate is the only one before main moves: a break it misses, the agents fix on main together. No end-to-end before a land: no `*_e2e.py`, `*_tmux.py`, `pnpm test:land`, Playwright or desktop harness check, as-he-does row, nor `gate.sh full`, even when a brief names one.
After the land, the tip gate runs run_all's e2e and tmux tests on main's tip, by hand on a release's cut (`scripts/nightly_gate.sh --tip` through heavy-run): its launchd jobs are off by Gabriel's choice, never re-install them or any login item; until then nothing checks main after you. The desktop Playwright checks, their coverage map and as-he-does run in the nightly and before a release, on its exact sha. Put your land sha in your report. A red there names you in tip-red, which main forwards: revert your land on main first, then fix and land again. Shots are signed after the land, on the landed sha. A release is cut only from a tip-green sha.
The full rule: docs/loop-speed.md §0, rule 11.
